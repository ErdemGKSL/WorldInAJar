# World In A Jar 1.1.0

World In A Jar 1.1.0 is the first stable storefront release.

## Highlights

- Craft, place, enter, pick up, and carry persistent miniature worlds.
- See live previews in both directions: the jar interior from outside and the surrounding world
  from inside.
- Move players, mobs, items, and projectiles through jar portals.
- Inspect a carried jar from the inventory while its holder continues moving.
- Attach jars into larger modular spaces and detach them without losing their interiors.
- Recover eligible lost jars with `/jar back`.
- Keep jar interiors isolated with configurable teleport and respawn protection.
- Insert portal-transferred items directly into attached hoppers.
- Improved preview performance with hidden-block culling, tick budgets, and optional Distant
  Horizons integration.

## Requirements

- Paper 1.21.11
- Java 21

ProtocolLib is optional and is needed only for the `protocol` entity-preview backend.
